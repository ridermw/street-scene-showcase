Concept and references.

A red performance car moves along a brick street. Fire escapes, parked traffic, asphalt, and directional afternoon light define the scene.

No separate drawn concept art was produced. These early Blender renders show the initial scene studies. The external video below supplied the visual reference.

Material sources

Vehicle and building geometry is original procedural work. Brick surfaces use ambientCG Bricks059. Asphalt uses ambientCG Asphalt012.

Both material sets use CC0 1.0. Their color, roughness, and displacement images were used in the scene.
